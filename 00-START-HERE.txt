🆘 IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Check logs:
   $ docker-compose logs -f console

2. Check containers:
   $ docker-compose ps

3. Restart services:
   $ docker-compose restart redis

4. Full reset (preserve data):
   $ docker-compose down
   $ docker-compose up -d --build

5. Emergency cleanup:
   $ docker-compose down -v  # ⚠️ Removes data!
